BACKGROUND / WHY IT MATTERS

Abstract mechanics can be difficult for young children to understand. Our team designed a Chinese-English STEM unit for Primary 1-2 learners, connecting rocket construction, interactive simulation and physical launches through a Hong Kong-Jiangxi service-learning project.

What we observed
Most learners could describe the basic launch principle. Construction, simulation and launch activities gave students several ways to connect pressure and force with visible motion.
What this suggests
The project combines science, engineering, computing and teaching in a service-learning setting. Its educational effectiveness needs systematic evaluation.

SIMULATION AS A LEARNING TOOL

The simulator makes parameter changes and predicted trajectories visible before launch. Students can explore cause and effect, then compare the model with a physical experiment. Predictions support discussion; they are not validated measurements.
